01SECURITY

What belongs in a practical security review?

A useful review looks at how people access systems, how devices are protected and what happens when something goes wrong. Start with the controls that reduce real risk and fit the way your organization works.

  • Review account access and multi-factor authentication.
  • Check device updates, endpoint protection and admin privileges.
  • Test whether backups can restore the information your team needs.
  • Give staff a clear way to report a suspicious message or incident.

02CONTINUITY

Could your business recover from an outage?

Backups matter, but recovery also depends on knowing what to restore first, who can make decisions and how your team will communicate if normal systems are unavailable.

  • Identify the systems and data essential to daily operations.
  • Agree recovery priorities and responsibilities.
  • Keep recovery instructions accessible when core services are offline.
  • Practice a restore and update the plan when your environment changes.

03CLOUD

Is your cloud setup keeping pace with your team?

Cloud services work best when ownership, access and ongoing costs are understood. A quick review can identify unused subscriptions, inconsistent permissions and changes that would make collaboration simpler.

  • Check who owns accounts, services and renewals.
  • Review access when roles change or people leave.
  • Confirm sharing settings suit the information being handled.
  • Plan migrations around real workflows, not only infrastructure.
